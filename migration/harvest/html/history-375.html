The Declaration of Laudium

Adopted at the Sixth Synod of the Reformed Church in Africa, October 1990

  • •
    The Biblical Gospel

    Every person is born in sin and lost without Christ, and salvation is found in no other name.

  • •
    The Holy Spirit's Witness

    Evangelism bears no fruit apart from the Spirit, and a holy life gives that witness its credibility.

  • •
    Evangelistic Witness & Compassionate Service

    The whole Gospel is proclaimed to the whole world, with real care for the poor and those in need.

  • •
    Unity

    Unity is affirmed with all born-again believers, and racism within the church is named a denial of the Gospel.

  • •
    Prophetic Witness

    The church speaks against injustice and oppression, while remaining free from any political ideology or movement.

This declaration is more than history — it's a promise our church stands on. It was personally presented to RCA Charisma on 25 April 2004, and it still shapes how we read Scripture, love our neighbours, and stand for truth today.

Presented to the Reformed Church in Africa, Charisma Congregation, 25 April 2004 — Rev PJP de Beer, Moderator.

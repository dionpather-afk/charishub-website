A Bible Reading Plan for You

God's Word is a lamp for our feet and a light for our path. Whether you're just beginning your walk with the Lord or have been in the Word for years, we'd love for you to journey through Scripture with us. Here's a simple daily rhythm to help you stay rooted in God's Word this week.

MondayPsalm 1 – 5
TuesdayProverbs 1 – 3
WednesdayMatthew 1 – 4
ThursdayGenesis 1 – 3
FridayJohn 1 – 3
SaturdayRomans 1 – 3
SundayPsalm 100; 103; 150 (Day of rest & worship)

A new weekly plan will be posted here regularly. If you'd like a printed copy for home or a longer plan to read through the whole Bible in a year, please reach out to us on the Contact page — we'd love to help you get started.

Our pastoral team is preparing weekly reflections to share with you soon — check back shortly, and God bless you in the meantime.
